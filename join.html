We are hiring curiosity, in both dialects.

If you like taking a system apart to find out why it is slow, or taking a model apart to find out why it is right for the wrong reason, there is a desk here for you.

Open · rolling

PhD students — both divisions

Admission runs through the department. Email the PI before you apply, and say which of the research threads you would want to start on and why.

Open · rolling

MS students

Suited to people who want a publishable result inside two years. Systems engineering experience or strong empirical instincts both work.

Open · by term

Undergraduate researchers & interns

Usually starting on evaluation tooling, reproductions or benchmark infrastructure — real jobs, and the fastest way to learn how the group thinks.

By enquiry

Postdocs & visiting researchers

Write to us with a one-page sketch of what you would do here. Funding depends on the cycle; good proposals have a way of finding it.

Applying

What to send

Short is fine. Specific is better than polished.

01

A CV — one or two pages, with any code you are proud of linked directly.

02

A paragraph naming a paper or research thread of ours and what you would question or extend in it.

03

Which division you lean toward — and it is a perfectly good answer to say you want to sit between them.

04

Transcripts or references only if your programme requires them. We would rather read your code.
