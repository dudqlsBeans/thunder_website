Welcome to THUNDER Research Group

THUNDER Research Group belongs to both the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and the Graduate School of AI at Seoul National University. Our goal is to accelerate the training and inference of large-scale AI models and to reduce their size. We also work on natural language processing from the perspective of deep learning. We emphasise pragmatic approaches.

Departments

Accelerator-based heterogeneous systems are the de facto standard for running large-scale applications, but the programming wall stands between a model designer and the machine. We work on both sides of it.

COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

Execution platforms for deep learning and quantum computing

Compilers, runtime systems and programming environments for GPUs, FPGAs and clusters — built so that a model designer does not have to know the machine to use all of it.

  • 01Heterogeneous accelerators — communication across mixed NVIDIA and AMD GPUs, CUDA Unified Memory, and AI computation on NPUs and FPGAs.
  • 02Kernel-level optimization — Tensor Core FFT and convolution, GEMM on NPUs, and FPGA–GPU hybrid execution.
  • 03Distributed inference — MoE serving, cluster energy tracking, and scheduling tuned to real usage patterns.
  • 04Quantum–classical hybrid — exact simulation of large circuits on classical resources, with storage offloading.
Graduate School of AI

Large language models, in Korean

Datasets, tokenizers and models for a language the leaderboards were not built for — plus the evaluation needed to say whether any of it actually worked.

  • 01Efficient generation — parallel token generation, diffusion language models, and tokenizers that cut token counts.
  • 02Long context & multimodal — positional encoding and attention sinks, extended to medical imaging, ECG and EMR.
  • 03Evaluation — Korean benchmarks, and negation understanding in language and vision-language models.
  • 04Domain-specific models — legal LLMs, de-identification, RAG over statutes, and clinical biomarkers.
