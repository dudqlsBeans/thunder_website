01  /  Divisions

Two halves of the same question

What can be computed, and what does it take to actually run it. One division builds the substrate; the other pushes the models that stress it. Neither is much use alone.

DIVISION 01  —  COMPUTER SCIENCE

Execution platforms for deep learning and quantum computing

Compilers, runtimes and schedulers that turn a program into work a machine will finish — measured end to end, on the hardware, not in the abstract.

  • 01Compilers & runtimes — graph capture, kernel selection, memory planning for deep learning.
  • 02Distributed execution — parallelism strategy, communication scheduling, elastic training and serving.
  • 03Quantum toolchains — circuit compilation, mapping and routing, noise-aware scheduling and control.
  • 04Hybrid orchestration — classical and quantum resources under one scheduler and one cost model.
DIVISION 02  —  DATA SCIENCE

Large language models and multimodal intelligence

How models learn, reason and fail across text, speech and vision — and what has to change in the recipe when a modality is added rather than bolted on.

  • 01Language models — pre- and post-training recipes, reasoning behaviour, alignment under real constraints.
  • 02Multimodal learning — speech, vision and text in one representation, and where the alignment breaks.
  • 03Efficiency — data curation, distillation, and inference cost as a first-class research variable.
  • 04Evaluation — benchmarks that survive contact with deployment, and honest failure analysis.
02  /  Current work

Six threads we are pulling on

Active directions across both divisions. Each one is a standing project with students, code and a benchmark attached.

01

Compiler stacks for deep learning

Scheduling, fusion and memory planning that hold up across model families instead of one benchmark. We care about the compile-time / runtime boundary and what belongs on each side.

02

Distributed training & serving

Parallelism strategies, collective communication and elasticity for clusters that are shared, heterogeneous and occasionally unreliable — which is to say, real ones.

Quantum compilation & control

Mapping and routing under real device topologies, noise-aware scheduling, and the software layer between a circuit and the pulses that run it.

Data curation, post-training and the reasoning behaviour it produces — including the parts that look like capability but are closer to memorisation.

Multimodal representation
