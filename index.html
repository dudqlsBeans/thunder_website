Accelerating large-scale AI, pragmatically.

THUNDER belongs to both the Department of Computer Science and Engineering in the College of Engineering and the Department of Data Science in the Graduate School of Data Science at Seoul National University. Our goal is to accelerate the training and inference of large-scale AI models and to reduce their size. We also work on natural language processing from the perspective of deep learning. We emphasise pragmatic approaches.

01  /  Departments

One group, two departments

Accelerator-based heterogeneous systems are the de facto standard for running large-scale applications, but the programming wall stands between a model designer and the machine. We work on both sides of it.

COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

Execution platforms for deep learning and quantum computing

Compilers, runtime systems and programming environments for GPUs, FPGAs and clusters — built so that a model designer does not have to know the machine to use all of it.

  • 01Automatic parallelization — finding the best parallelization scheme for a DNN on multi-node, multi-GPU systems.
  • 02Memory beyond the GPU — DeepUM and SnuRHAC use CUDA Unified Memory, host memory and SSDs to run past GPU capacity.
  • 03Frameworks for FPGAs — SOFF synthesises OpenCL and CUDA for FPGAs without user annotations.
  • 04Quantum simulation — SnuQS scales full-state circuit simulation using storage devices.
DATA SCIENCE

Large language models, in Korean

Datasets, tokenizers and models for a language the leaderboards were not built for — plus the evaluation needed to say whether any of it actually worked.

  • 01Korean datasets — web crawling, de-identification and de-duplication at scale for LLM training.
  • 02Korean tokenizers — Thunder-Tok and morpheme-aware tokenization that cuts tokens per word.
  • 03Korean language models — Thunder-LLM, adapted to Korean with minimal resources.
  • 04Benchmarks — Thunder-NUBench and Thunder-KoNUBench for what the models still cannot do.

Four areas of research

Standing projects, each with students, code and published results behind it. The research page describes them in full.

01

Execution platforms for deep learning

Automatic parallelization of DNN models across multi-node, multi-GPU systems; DeepUM and SnuRHAC for memory and cluster transparency; DeepCuts for kernel generation; and a full-stack deep learning framework for FPGAs.

ParallelizationUnified memoryFPGAKernels
02

Large language models

Korean datasets, tokenizers and language models built from scratch, along with the training techniques, energy characterisation and compression needed to make them affordable.

Korean dataTokenizersCompressionEnergy
03

Quantum computing

An end-to-end software stack — compiler, runtime and simulator. SnuQS simulated a 42-qubit quantum supremacy circuit on a workstation with NVMe SSDs and HDDs, 256 times larger than a conventional full-state simulator.

SnuQSFull-state simulationStorage I/O
04

Under-display camera image restoration

Cameras placed beneath a display lose light and gain diffraction. We build real-world datasets — UDC-SIT and UDC-VIT — and deep learning restoration methods that work on them rather than on synthetic degradation.
